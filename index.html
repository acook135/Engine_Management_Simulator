How it works

  • 🗣Listen to the customer, then start the car and see for yourself.
  • 💻Scan for fault codes. They tell you where to look, not what to replace.
  • 🧪Test — click a part in the engine bay, then click pins to place probes.
  • 🔧Fix only what you have proved is faulty.
  • ✅Confirm the repair and collect your stars.
Scoring: start on 100. Hints −5, unnecessary part −10, wrong diagnosis −10, and −1 per minute after 15 minutes.
